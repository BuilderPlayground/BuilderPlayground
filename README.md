# Hi there, I'm Gina! 👋

## About Me

I'm an interdisciplinary software engineer with a background in behavioral science, marketing, and entrepreneurship, and a strong interest in user research and product design.

A question has followed me across disciplines: **What becomes possible when we build better conditions for people to thrive?**

That question informs how I build. I'm interested in understanding—and improving—the conditions under which people can exercise agency and actualize potential. That means looking beyond whether a product works to the behavior, context, incentives, and systems surrounding it.
